Privacy policy

Your books stay yours.

Effective October 27, 2026

What Clip stores

Clip stores your imported books, audio, alignment data, playback position, and app settings on your devices and in Clip’s private iCloud Drive container. Clip does not operate an account system, advertising network, or analytics service.

What leaves the app

Transcription runs on your Mac by default. If you choose OpenRouter cloud alignment, Clip uploads audiobook audio to OpenRouter and its transcription providers using your account. EPUB matching stays on your Mac. Your OpenRouter API key is stored in this Mac’s Keychain and sent only to OpenRouter.

When you choose to export a highlight, Clip sends the selected passage and its book details to Readwise using the Readwise API. Your Readwise access token is stored in Apple Keychain and is sent only to Readwise.

Third-party services

Apple processes data used by iCloud under Apple’s privacy policy. Readwise processes exported highlights under Readwise’s privacy policy. Marginalia receives highlights only through a connection you set up with your Readwise account; Clip does not send data to Marginalia directly. OpenRouter and its selected providers process cloud transcription audio under their own privacy and data policies.

Apple privacy Readwise privacy OpenRouter privacy

Your choices

You can remove books from Clip, delete Clip’s files from iCloud Drive, remove your Readwise token in Clip, or revoke the token in Readwise. To delete highlights already exported, use your Readwise account controls. You can choose On this Mac to keep transcription local and remove your OpenRouter key in Clip Settings.

Children and changes

Clip is not directed to children under 13. If this policy changes, the updated policy and effective date will be posted here.

# App Review notes — Clip 1.0

Clip does not host, sell, or distribute books. It imports DRM-free ebooks and
audiobooks that the user has the right to use.

## iPhone/iPad review path

1. Download the original Clip demo package from the Support URL supplied in
   this submission.
2. In Files, tap the downloaded ZIP to expand it.
3. Tap `Clip-Demo.clipbook`; choose Clip if iOS asks which app should open it.
4. In Clip, open Settings and paste the dedicated Readwise review token supplied
   in the secure demo credential field.
5. Open the demo book and tap Play.
6. While audio is playing, tap the terracotta Clip button. The app matches the
   just-heard audio to the ebook text and sends that passage to Readwise.
7. The same action can be invoked hands-free with the App Shortcut phrase
   “Clip that.”
8. Tap Read to switch to the synchronized reading view. Tap any sentence to seek
   audio, or long-press and drag across sentences to clip a selected passage.

## Mac review path

1. Open Clip for Mac.
2. Drop a DRM-free EPUB and the matching audiobook onto the two labeled drop
   zones.
3. Follow the on-screen steps. The first alignment downloads an on-device
   Whisper model, so it can take several minutes.
4. When processing completes, the `.clipbook` package is written to Clip's
   iCloud Drive container and appears in the iPhone/iPad app.

## Accounts and network behavior

- Clip has no app-specific account or server.
- A Readwise account is required only to export a highlight.
- The Readwise token is stored in Apple Keychain and sent only to Readwise.
- Marginalia receives no data directly from Clip. Users may connect Marginalia
  to the same Readwise account themselves.
- Imported books, audio, alignment data, and playback position stay on device or
  in Clip's private iCloud Drive container.
- Clip contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking SDK.

## Description

Clip turns listening into highlights you can actually revisit.

Pair a DRM-free ebook with its matching audiobook in Clip for Mac. Clip aligns
the words and audio, then sends the synced book to your iPhone or iPad through
iCloud. Listen, read, and move between the two without losing your place.

When a line lands, tap Clip or say “Clip that.” Clip finds the exact passage you
just heard and sends it to your Readwise account, where it is ready to flow into
Marginalia alongside the rest of your reading.

With Clip you can:

• Pair a DRM-free EPUB and audiobook on Mac
• Listen and read from the same place on iPhone and iPad
• Follow the spoken passage in a focused reading view
• Tap any sentence to seek the audio
• Clip a passage with one tap or the phrase “Clip that”
• Export exact ebook text—not a rough transcript—to Readwise
• Keep listening when you are offline and retry pending highlights later

Clip has no account, ads, analytics, or tracking. Your books stay on your devices
and in Clip's private iCloud Drive container. A Readwise account is required to
export highlights.

Clip is an independent app and is not affiliated with Readwise or Marginalia.
Use only DRM-free media that you have the right to use.

## Content rights

The app displays third-party content only when a user imports their own
DRM-free ebook and matching audiobook. Clip does not supply, host, sell, or
remove DRM from book content. Set `usesThirdPartyContent` to **true** and
confirm that users are instructed to import only media they have the right to
use.
